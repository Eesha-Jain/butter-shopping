# Butter Shopping

Butter is a mobile-first grocery shopping app designed to help students and shared households plan affordable, practical grocery trips. Based on sales and anticipated discounts later in the week, the app automatically picks which store has the cheapest product / amount ratio and picks when your grocery days are to minimize the amount that you pay. It brings product discovery, shopping lists, nearby stores, and voice-assisted list management together in one place, with dietary preferences and local price information built into the shopping experience.

The app is built with **TypeScript, React Native, and Expo**. Its data services use **Supabase and PostgreSQL**, with a **Node.js/JavaScript** service for catalog and price collection and **SQL** migrations for database changes.

## Features

### Find products and build a list

- Browse a product catalog by category or search by product and brand.
- Narrow results using food preferences, sale status, and price sorting.
- Open product details, choose a quantity, and add items directly to a list.
- Keep favorites and frequently used staples close at hand.
- Import recipes from pasted text, a web link, or a photo, review the ingredient matches, and add selected ingredients to a list.

### Shop with local context

- Explore nearby grocery stores on a map and choose preferred stores.
- Use saved locations or device location to find stores and resolve the shopping area.
- See prices and promotions when catalog data is available for a product and location.
- Submit product and price information through supported reporting flows to help keep local data useful.

### Plan with other people

- Maintain personal and shared grocery lists, and switch the active list while shopping.
- Create or join a household or group using an invite code.
- Share group invitations using the system share sheet.
- Use guest mode to explore core shopping flows without signing in, then continue with an account when needed.

### Use voice to manage a list

- Speak a grocery request and review recognized items before applying changes.
- Add, update, remove, or swap list items through the voice shopping flow.
- Use typed text as an alternative input.
- Receive spoken assistant responses where supported.
